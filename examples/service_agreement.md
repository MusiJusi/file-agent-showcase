# Meridian Reporting — Service agreement

This is a fictional sample document for demonstrating File Agent.
It is not a real contract.

## Availability credits

A credit is a percentage of the monthly service fee.
Only one availability tier applies to a measurement period.

| Measured availability | Service credit |
| --- | ---: |
| At least 99.9% | 0% |
| At least 99.0% and below 99.9% | 5% |
| At least 95.0% and below 99.0% | 10% |
| Below 95.0% | 20% |

## Claim procedure

Submit a claim within 15 calendar days after the end of the affected month.
Include the service account, measurement period, and monitoring evidence.

## Exclusions

Scheduled maintenance announced at least 48 hours in advance is excluded from
the availability calculation.
